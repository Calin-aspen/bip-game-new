# 🔴 BIP

> *"Hey! Press me! I promise it'll be fun!"*

A small pixel-art roguelike made in **Godot 4.6** about one big red button named Bip.
Every press does something random: coins, bombs, monsters, strange notes on the floor...
Bip is your best friend. At first.

## How to play
- **Click Bip** or press **SPACE / ENTER** to press the button
- The **FUN** bar fills with every press: more fun means bigger rewards *and* bigger dangers
- Press **STOP** to finish a room and cash in a bonus before Bip overloads
- Beat 3 floors and their bosses. If you fall asleep, you wake up and try again
- **ESC** pauses, **F11** toggles fullscreen

There are **3 endings** to find.

## Running it
1. Install [Godot 4.6](https://godotengine.org/download)
2. Open Godot → **Import** → select `project.godot`
3. Press **F5**
